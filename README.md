## 🌾 Overview

**FasalSetu** provides intelligent, data-driven decision support for Indian farmers:
1. **Crop Recommendation Engine**: Analyzes soil nutrients (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium) and local climate metrics (Temperature, Humidity, Soil pH, Rainfall) before planting to recommend the top crop + alternatives with statistical confidence.
2. **Market Price Comparison Engine**: Enables farmers to compare live/recent commodity prices across nearby agricultural mandis (markets) for any of the 22 supported crops, filter by state (case-insensitively), and identify the market offering the best return.
3. **Farmer Q&A Assistant**: Single-turn regional-language question answering endpoint powered by Groq's high-speed inference (`llama-3.1-8b-instant`). Farmers can ask pest control, sowing timing, fertilizer usage, and general crop care questions in Hindi, English, and regional languages and receive immediate, structured advice in the same language with safety disclaimers.
4. **Voice Input for Farmer Q&A (Feature 3B)**: Enables farmers to submit voice recordings (`.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.webm`) of their questions instead of typing. Transcribed using Groq Whisper (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`) with an explicit 15-second timeout, then answered by the Q&A engine in the farmer's language with safety disclaimers.
5. **Conversational Follow-ups (Feature 4)**: Enables farmers to ask follow-up questions referencing prior dialogue within the same session. By providing an optional `session_id` (UUID generated by frontend), the backend injects up to 3 previous Q&A turns in chronological order into Groq's prompt context, with previous answers truncated at 300 characters for token efficiency. Works seamlessly across both text and voice endpoints, with graceful stateless fallback when `session_id` is omitted or unrecorded.
6. **Image-based Crop Diagnosis (Feature 5)**: Enables farmers to upload a photograph of a diseased or damaged crop/leaf (`.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`, up to 10MB) and receive an instant visual diagnosis, confidence note, actionable treatment steps, and safety disclaimer in their language. Powered by Groq's high-performance vision model (`qwen/qwen3.6-27b`), complete with sliding window rate limiting (10 req/min), dedicated database audit logging (`CropDiagnosisLog`), and graceful fallback (503) if the preview model is decommissioned or temporarily unavailable.
7. **Price Negotiation Engine & Buyer Marketplace (Features 7 & 10)**: Real JWT authenticated agricultural marketplace. Farmers list crops with asking prices, buyers discover open listings (filtered by status and crop), submit offers, and negotiate in auditable threads. Enforces role-based permissions, listing ownership checks, negotiation party authorization (403 for third parties), real foreign key constraints to `users.id`, and personal dashboards (`GET /api/my/offers` for buyers and `GET /api/my/listings` for farmers).
8. **Answer Feedback Loop (Feature 8)**: Enables farmers and reviewers to submit thumbs-up (`"up"`) or thumbs-down (`"down"`) feedback and optional comments (up to 500 characters) on any AI-generated response across Text Q&A, Voice Q&A, and Image Diagnosis. Implements idempotent overwrite behavior for seamless correction of accidental taps, returns the row's `log_id` across all AI response schemas, and persists ratings without extra joins.
9. **Real Authentication for Both Roles (Feature 9)**: Provides phone + password registration and login for both `farmer` and `buyer` roles with bcrypt hashing and JWT bearer tokens (`python-jose`). Supports dual-role accounts on the same phone number via a composite unique constraint `(phone, role)`, enforces anti-enumeration uniform `401 Unauthorized` responses on failed logins, and offers `get_current_user` and `require_role(...)` dependencies for route protection.
10. **AI Negotiation Advisor (Feature 11)**: Provides strategic, real-time negotiation coaching to farmers and buyers on active listing threads (`POST /api/listings/{id}/negotiation-chat`). Grounded in recorded mandi price benchmarks (framed as national market data across recorded mandis) and the listing's chronological offer history. Operates strictly in an advisory capacity (the AI never submits or accepts offers autonomously), enforces shared party authorization (`verify_negotiation_party` returns 403 for non-parties), enforces sliding-window rate limiting (10 req/min per user), and logs every advisory exchange to `negotiation_chat_logs`.
11. **Water Irrigation Advisory (Feature 12)**: Rule-based soil moisture and irrigation urgency estimation based on live and forecast Open-Meteo weather data (`GET /api/irrigation-advisory`). In the absence of IoT soil sensor hardware, deterministically estimates moisture percentages and actionable urgency tiers (`urgent`, `monitor`, `ok`) by combining past 3-day rainfall, current weather, and 3-day precipitation forecasts with canonical crop water requirements and soil drainage modifiers. Explicitly labeled as an agronomic estimate (`"is_estimate": true`) with zero LLM hallucinations or extra model overhead.
12. **Yield & Harvest Estimate (Feature 13)**: Rule-based crop yield and harvest timeline projection based on land size, sowing date, and published ICAR agronomic benchmarks (`GET /api/yield-estimate`). Aligns with the frontend `mockYieldData.ts` contract to project expected yield ranges (quintals), growth cycle progress percentage, calendar harvest window dates, human-friendly ETA bucketing, and mandi-grounded value and net profit projections (incorporating crop category cultivation cost ratios).
13. **Farm Profile & Operations (Feature 14)**: Farm profile and operational preference storage and retrieval matching the frontend's `FarmDetailsScreen` (onboarding creation via `POST /api/farm-profile`) and `ProfileScreen` (profile viewing via `GET /api/farm-profile` and partial editing via `PUT /api/farm-profile`). Authenticated via Feature 9 JWT, strictly role-restricted to farmers (`require_role("farmer")`, returning 403 Forbidden for buyers), and enforcing a 1-to-1 farmer-to-profile relationship at the database level (unique constraint on `farm_profiles.user_id`, returning 409 Conflict on duplicates). Validates crops against the canonical 22 crops (rejecting wheat/sugarcane with 422 naming valid crops), and provides a helper endpoint `GET /api/valid-crops` to eliminate frontend/backend drift.
14. **Government Role & Authentication Wiring (Feature 15a)**: Extends the authentication system to support `"government"` as a first-class user role alongside `"farmer"` and `"buyer"`, with open phone+password self-signup and token issuance. Establishes the foundation and `require_role("government")` dependency for future administrative features (Features 15b/15c/15d: Risk Map, Reports, and Interventions) with full triple-role coexistence on the same phone number.
15. **Government Command Center Risk Map & Hotspots API (Feature 15b)**: Provides government-authenticated spatial risk visibility matching the frontend command center (`mockCommandCenterData.ts`). Serves 17 rank-ordered agricultural hotspots with severity filters (`crop`, `risk_level`, `time_range`), hotspot detail with full intervention lists and multi-period trend arrays, 17 stylized zone map markers, 11 district zone geometry bounding boxes, and static filter reference options. Strictly authenticated via `require_role("government")` (403 Forbidden for farmer/buyer roles). Explicitly documented as synthetic seeded demo data (see Section 18 for full architecture details and future telemetry/diagnosis-aggregation roadmap).
16. **Government Command Center Report Generation (Feature 15c)**: Generates and serves official state surveillance reports in PDF, Excel (.xlsx), and CSV formats (`/api/government/reports`). Grounded in real hotspot surveillance data for Risk Summary and Crop Health reports, while explicitly flagging missing upstream telemetry models for Yield Forecast and Intervention Log as demo placeholders (`is_placeholder_content: true`). Generates real physical files on local disk (`backend/storage/reports/`), calculates authentic byte counts (2KB - 10KB), enforces dynamic district validation against the database, seeds 24 historical reports across 17 Telangana districts, and streams file downloads with RFC-compliant MIME types.
17. **Buyer Company Profile (Feature 16a)**: Enterprise profile and reputation management for authenticated buyers matching the frontend's `BuyerOffer` identity fields (`company_name`, `verified`, `rating`, `review_count`, `tags`, `avatar_initial`, `avatar_color`). Enforces a 1-to-1 buyer-to-profile relationship via unique constraint on `buyer_profiles.user_id`, validates operational tags against a canonical 6-tag vocabulary, and derives avatar initials and deterministic palette colors at runtime. Implements explicit PUT field whitelisting (silently ignoring attempts to modify `verified` or seeded ratings) and exposes a privacy-respecting public endpoint (`GET /api/buyer-profile/{buyer_user_id}`) that omits exact GPS coordinates for viewing by negotiating counterparties.
18. **Transaction Status & Lifecycle Tracking (Feature 17)**: Post-acceptance deal lifecycle tracking matching the frontend `TransactionStatusScreen`'s stage timeline once a Feature 7/10 offer is accepted (listing status becomes `sold`). Automatically creates a `Transaction` record with pre-completed `matched` and `negotiated` stages, active `logistics`, and pending `payment` and `completed` stages. Provides manual stage advancements (`confirm_pickup`, `confirm_payment`) performed by the owning farmer, strictly enforcing progression order (HTTP 409 Conflict if premature or re-advanced after completion) and shared negotiation party authorization. Personal view endpoint (`GET /api/my/transactions`) allows farmers and buyers to monitor their active and completed deals.
19. **Government Interventions Management (Feature 15d)**: Administrative agricultural crisis containment operations and Kanban-style status tracking on the Government Command Center dashboard (`/api/government/interventions`). Persists interventions across Telangana districts with severity ratings, affected hectares, farmer counts, team allocation (`Field Team 1–6`), and multi-entry activity audit logs. Aggregates statewide containment metrics (`/api/government/interventions/stats`) and synchronizes real-time status transitions with the frontend Command Center Interventions board.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Docker Compose)

The entire stack (FastAPI Backend + PostgreSQL Database) launches with a single command.

### 1. Launch Services
```bash
# From project root:
docker-compose up --build
```

- **Backend API**: [http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)
- **Interactive Swagger Docs**: [http://localhost:8000/docs](http://localhost:8000/docs)
- **ReDoc Documentation**: [http://localhost:8000/redoc](http://localhost:8000/redoc)
- **PostgreSQL**: `localhost:5432` (`fasalsetu_db`)

> **Startup Ordering Guarantee**: Docker Compose enforces `condition: service_healthy` on the PostgreSQL container (`fasalsetu-db`). The backend starts only after PostgreSQL is fully initialized and accepting connections.
>
> **Build-Time ML Model Training**: The Random Forest model is trained at **Docker build time** inside the Dockerfile (`RUN python -m app.ml.train_crop_model`), baking `crop_rf_model.joblib` into the container image. Container startup only loads the pre-baked artifact into memory.
>
> **Automatic Database Seeding**: On first startup, the application initializes all database tables (`init_db`) and automatically seeds 20 mandis and commodity prices across 5 states (`seed_market_prices`).

## 🗄️ Database Configuration & SQLite Boundary

- **Production & Hackathon Demo**: The application connects to **PostgreSQL** (`postgresql://postgres:postgres@db:5432/fasalsetu_db`).
- **SQLite Usage Boundary**: SQLite is **strictly isolated to the automated test suite** (`tests/conftest.py`) using in-memory sessions (`sqlite:///:memory:`). Tests NEVER touch or modify the PostgreSQL database.
- **Local Development without Docker**: If running locally on host Python, you can point to local PostgreSQL or set `DATABASE_URL=sqlite:///./fasalsetu_dev.db` in your local `.env`.
- **Groq API Configuration**: Set `GROQ_API_KEY` in `.env` to enable the Farmer Q&A Assistant and Whisper Voice Transcription. Automated tests mock all Groq client calls and do not consume credits or require a live key.

### 2. Crop Recommendation (Feature 1)
Predicts the optimal crop and top 2 alternatives based on 7 soil and climate parameters.

- **Method**: `POST`
- **Path**: `/api/crop-recommendation`
- **Headers**: `Content-Type: application/json`

#### Request Body Specification:
| Field | Type | Required | Constraints | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `nitrogen` | float | Yes | — | Soil Nitrogen content (N ratio) |
| `phosphorus` | float | Yes | — | Soil Phosphorus content (P ratio) |
| `potassium` | float | Yes | — | Soil Potassium content (K ratio) |
| `temperature` | float | Yes | — | Ambient temperature in °C |
| `humidity` | float | Yes | `0.0` to `100.0` | Relative humidity percentage |
| `ph` | float | Yes | `0.0` to `14.0` | Soil pH level |
| `rainfall` | float | Yes | — | Rainfall in mm |

#### Example Request Payload:
```json
{
  "nitrogen": 90.0,
  "phosphorus": 42.0,
  "potassium": 43.0,
  "temperature": 20.87,
  "humidity": 82.0,
  "ph": 6.5,
  "rainfall": 202.93
}
```

#### Success Response (200 OK):
```json
{
  "recommended_crop": "rice",
  "confidence": 0.90,
  "alternatives": [
    {
      "crop": "jute",
      "confidence": 0.10
    },
    {
      "crop": "pomegranate",
      "confidence": 0.00
    }
  ]
}
```

#### Validation Error (422 Unprocessable Content):
```json
{
  "detail": "Input validation failed",
  "errors": [
    {
      "field": "ph",
      "message": "ph must be between 0 and 14"
    }
```

### 6. Conversational Follow-ups (Feature 4)
Allows farmers to ask follow-up questions referencing previous answers in the same dialogue session instead of treating every question statelessly.

#### How It Works:
1. **Frontend-Managed Session Lifecycle**:
   - On the first question in a session, the client can omit `session_id` (or pass a new UUID).
   - The backend auto-generates a UUID `session_id` if omitted and returns it in the response.
   - For all subsequent follow-up questions, the client sends this `session_id` back in the request body (text) or form data (voice).
2. **Context Window & History Cap**:
   - The backend queries the last `N=3` (`MAX_CONVERSATION_HISTORY = 3`) turns from `FarmerQALog` for the provided `session_id`.
   - History turns are ordered **chronologically from oldest to newest** so the conversation flows naturally before the current question.
   - Previous answers are truncated at 300 characters (`MAX_HISTORY_ANSWER_LENGTH = 300`) to preserve tokens for the answer while maintaining context.
3. **Stateless Fallback**:
   - If `session_id` is missing or has no prior logs in the database, the endpoint processes the question statelessly without errors and returns a valid `session_id`.
4. **Voice Endpoint Parity**:
   - `POST /api/farmer-qa/voice` supports the exact same `session_id` form field, enabling voice follow-ups to text queries and vice versa.

#### Multi-Turn Interaction Walkthrough:

##### Turn 1 — Initial Question (Omits `session_id`):
```bash
curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/api/farmer-qa \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "question": "What is the recommended fertilizer for wheat?",
    "language": "english"
  }'
```
**Response (200 OK)**:
```json

  "answer": "Apply 120 kg Nitrogen, 60 kg Phosphorus, and 40 kg Potassium per hectare. Use Urea and DAP as the primary sources.",
  "language_used": "English",

  "is_farming_related": true,
  "session_id": "8f31b81e-9274-4bf8-b2a6-9818816c4f1c"
}
```

##### Turn 2 — Follow-up Question (Supplies `session_id`):
```bash

  -d '{
    "question": "When should I apply the Nitrogen?",
    "session_id": "8f31b81e-9274-4bf8-b2a6-9818816c4f1c",
    "language": "english"
  }'
```
**Response (200 OK)**:

### 7. Price Negotiation & Buyer Marketplace Browse (Features 7 & 10)
Enables an authentic, JWT-secured agricultural marketplace between farmers and buyers. Farmers list crops with asking prices, buyers browse active market listings, submit offers, and engage in multi-round counter-negotiations until an agreement is reached or rejected.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Feature 10 Auth Wiring & Security Enforcements**:
> - **Zero Loose Identifiers**: `farmer_id` and `buyer_id` are **completely removed** from request bodies. Identities are derived strictly from authenticated JWT bearer tokens (`current_user.id`).
> - **Farmer Listing Ownership**: Creating a listing requires `role="farmer"`. A farmer cannot create a listing under another person's ID. Countering or acting on a listing requires the farmer to actually own that listing (**403 Forbidden** if not).
> - **Negotiation Party Authorization**: Only the legitimate recipient party is authorized to accept or reject an offer (**403 Forbidden** for third parties or the offer creator).
>   - Buyer offers can only be accepted/rejected by the listing's owning farmer.
>   - Farmer counter-offers can only be accepted/rejected by the specific buyer involved on that negotiation thread.
> - **Buyer Thread Continuity**: A buyer cannot counter into another buyer's private negotiation thread on a listing (**403 Forbidden**).

#### Marketplace Endpoints:
| Method | Path | Auth Required | Role | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `POST` | `/api/listings` | **Yes** | `farmer` | Create a new crop listing (`farmer_id` set from token) |
| `GET` | `/api/listings` | Optional | Any | Browse marketplace listings (`status` defaults to `open`) |
| `GET` | `/api/listings/{id}` | Optional | Any | View listing details with complete chronological negotiation thread |
| `POST` | `/api/listings/{id}/offers` | **Yes** | `buyer` / `farmer` | Submit initial offer (buyer) or counter-offer (buyer or owning farmer) |
| `PATCH` | `/api/listings/{id}/offers/{offer_id}` | **Yes** | Legitimate Party | Accept or reject a pending offer (403 for unauthorized parties) |
| `GET` | `/api/my/offers` | **Yes** | `buyer` | Returns all offers made by authenticated buyer across all listings |
| `GET` | `/api/my/listings` | **Yes** | `farmer` | Returns all listings owned by authenticated farmer with full offer threads |

#### Database Foreign Key Constraints & Migration Notice:
- `CropListing.farmer_id` and `CropOffer.buyer_id` are configured as proper `Integer, ForeignKey("users.id", ondelete="CASCADE")`.
- **One-Time Migration Cleanup Notice**: Any legacy database rows created under early unauthenticated schemas with string IDs (e.g. `"farmer_01"`) will not map to integer user IDs. In local environments, legacy rows in `fasalsetu_dev.db` must be cleared or migrated before running.

#### Marketplace Browse & Location Gap Flag:
- `GET /api/listings` defaults to `status="open"` (excluding `sold` and `withdrawn` listings by default). Pass `status=all` to see listings across all states.
- **Flagged Gap**: Farmer location (state/district) is not currently captured in the `User` profile model (`users` table captures phone, role, password, and name only). Geographic filtering is deliberately deferred until user profile location fields are formally added.

#### State Machine & Validation Rules:
1. **Listing Status**:
   - `open`: Initial state on creation.
   - `negotiating`: Automatically set when an initial buyer offer is submitted.
   - `sold`: Deal closed when either party accepts a pending offer.
   - `withdrawn`: Terminal state if listing is cancelled.
2. **Offer Status**:
   - `pending`: Active offer awaiting response.
   - `countered`: Marked when superseded by a new counter-offer referencing `parent_offer_id`.
   - `accepted`: Deal closed; triggers listing status $\rightarrow$ `sold`.
   - `rejected`: Offer declined; listing status reverts to `open`.
3. **Safety Guardrails & Status Codes**:
   - **401 Unauthorized**: Missing or invalid JWT Bearer token on protected routes.
   - **403 Forbidden**: Role mismatch (e.g. buyer attempting to create a listing), unowned listing action, or third party attempting to accept/reject.
   - **409 Conflict**: Submitting an offer on a `sold` or `withdrawn` listing.
   - **422 Unprocessable Content**: Acting on a stale/countered offer, or non-positive offer amount.

### 9. Authentication & Authorization (Features 9 & 15a)
Provides phone number + password registration and login for `farmer`, `buyer`, and `government` roles. Issues signed JWT bearer access tokens (`python-jose`), hashes passwords using `bcrypt`, and protects role-restricted endpoints via `require_role(...)`.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Demo Simplification Disclosure: Open Government Self-Signup**:
> Open self-signup for the `"government"` role via `POST /api/auth/signup` is an MVP demo simplification designed to facilitate end-to-end testing of future administrative workflows (Features 15b/15c/15d). In a real-world governmental or institutional deployment, government accounts would be provisioned through official identity verification, institutional domain email checks, and administrative approval workflows rather than unverified self-registration.

#### Endpoints:
1. `POST /api/auth/signup` — Register a new account (`farmer`, `buyer`, or `government`)
2. `POST /api/auth/login` — Authenticate and obtain JWT token
3. `GET /api/auth/me` — Inspect profile of the authenticated token holder

#### Multi-Role Phone Architecture Decision:
- Accounts are uniquely constrained on **`(phone, role)`**, NOT on `phone` alone.
- This allows a user to register a `farmer` account, a `buyer` account, AND a `government` account with the same phone number.
- Because of this, `role` (`"farmer"`, `"buyer"`, or `"government"`) is required on both `/signup` and `/login` to specify which account is being accessed.

#### Anti-Enumeration Security:
- Failed login attempts due to wrong password or non-existent phone/role return an identical **`401 Unauthorized`** response (`{"detail": "Invalid phone, password, or role"}`).
- This prevents malicious users from enumerating registered phone numbers.

#### Startup Security Enforcement:
- `JWT_SECRET_KEY` has **no working default** in `config.py`.
- The application refuses to boot at startup if `JWT_SECRET_KEY` is missing, shorter than 16 characters, or matches any known insecure placeholder string.

### 11. AI Negotiation Advisor (Feature 11)
Provides real-time strategic negotiation guidance to farmers and buyers on active listing threads (`POST /api/listings/{id}/negotiation-chat`).

#### Key Architectural Guarantees:
1. **Strictly Advisory — Zero Autonomous Actions**: The assistant suggests fair counter-offer amounts, price margin analysis, and market context. It **never** submits, counters, accepts, or rejects an offer on anyone's behalf.
2. **National Benchmark Mandi Data Grounding**: When real mandi prices exist in the database for `listing.crop`, the assistant uses them as national benchmark ranges and average modal rates. It explicitly phrases this context as national/general market data rather than claiming to reflect the farmer's specific local mandi. If no records exist, it provides general agricultural pricing principles while reminding the user to check local spot rates.
3. **Shared Party Authorization (`verify_negotiation_party`)**: Only parties to the negotiation thread are authorized:
   - The listing's owning farmer (`int(listing.farmer_id) == int(current_user.id)`).
   - A buyer who has submitted an offer on the listing (`any(int(o.buyer_id) == int(current_user.id) for o in listing.offers)`).
   - All third parties are rejected with **`403 Forbidden`**.
4. **Per-User Sliding-Window Rate Limiting**: Enforces a strict limit of 10 requests per minute per user via `InMemoryRateLimiter`, returning **`429 Too Many Requests`** if exceeded.
5. **Decoupled Groq Service**: Implemented in `NegotiationAdvisorService` (independent from `FarmerQAService`). Handles upstream Groq timeouts or API failures with a clean **`503 Service Unavailable`** (`{"detail": "Negotiation assistant unavailable, please try again shortly"}`).
6. **Audit Trail Logging**: Every question, advisory response, and `market_context_used` flag is persisted to `negotiation_chat_logs`.

#### Request Specification (`POST /api/listings/{id}/negotiation-chat`):
- **Headers**: `Authorization: Bearer <JWT_TOKEN>`, `Content-Type: application/json`
- **Request Body**:
```json
{
  "message": "The buyer offered ₹2,150. Based on market prices, should I counter or accept?"
}
```

#### Response Specification (`200 OK`):
```json
{
  "reply": "National benchmark market data shows an average modal price of ₹2,250 across recorded mandis. The buyer's offer of ₹2,150 is ₹100 below this benchmark. You could consider countering at ₹2,200 to protect your margin while keeping the negotiation constructive.",
  "market_context_used": true
}
```

### 12. Water Irrigation Advisory (Feature 12)
Estimates soil moisture levels and recommends actionable irrigation urgency tiers based on real-time and forecasted Open-Meteo weather data (`GET /api/irrigation-advisory`).

#### Architectural Principles:
1. **Sensorless & Zero-Hardware**: In the absence of IoT soil moisture hardware, moisture status is estimated using past 3-day precipitation, current temperature, humidity, and 3-day forecasted rainfall.
2. **Deterministic Agronomic Rules**: Completely rule-based Python calculation with **zero LLM calls**, eliminating hallucination risks, API costs, and latency.
3. **Explicit Estimate Labeling**: Response explicitly contains `"is_estimate": true` and identifies `"data_source": "Open-Meteo (weather-based estimate, not a direct soil sensor reading)"`.
4. **Canonical 22 Crops & Soil Types**: Crop must match one of the canonical 22 supported crops (`VALID_CROPS_SET`). Unsupported crops (e.g. `wheat`) return `422 Unprocessable Content` naming the allowed list. Soil type defaults to `"loamy"` if omitted.
5. **Urgency Tiers & Action Rules**:
   - `urgent`: Moisture < 35% (or high water-demand crop < 45% under hot/dry conditions) — immediate irrigation recommended within 24–48 hours.
   - `monitor`: Moisture 35–65% — adequate moisture, reassess in 2–3 days.
   - `ok`: Moisture > 65% (or recent/forecast rain ≥ 15mm) — soil sufficiently hydrated, hold off on irrigation.
6. **External Service Resilience**: Open-Meteo requests are made via `httpx` (explicitly pinned in `requirements.txt`) with an explicit 10-second timeout, returning a clean HTTP 503 (`{"error": "Weather data unavailable, please try again shortly"}`) on failure.

| `longitude` | float | Yes | — | Field longitude coordinate (between -180.0 and 180.0) |

### 13. Yield & Harvest Estimate (Feature 13)
Computes benchmark-derived expected crop yield ranges, growth progress %, harvest calendar windows, human-friendly ETA bucketing, and market-grounded value and net profit projections (`GET /api/yield-estimate`).

#### Architectural Principles & Design Decisions:
1. **Sensorless & Remote-Sensing Free**: In the absence of on-field IoT soil/crop sensors or satellite remote sensing hardware, calculations are derived from verified agronomic benchmarks published by ICAR and State Agricultural Departments.
2. **Zero LLM Overhead**: 100% deterministic Python calculation eliminates hallucination risks, API costs, and latency.
3. **Mandi Market Grounding with Graceful Fallback**: Integrates Feature 2's `MarketPrice` table to price expected yield ranges and calculate per-acre profit. If no mandi records exist for that crop/state, it gracefully returns `price_data_available: false` and sets value/profit fields to `null` without error.
4. **Compounded Profit Estimate Caveat**: `projected_profit_per_acre` is a compounded projection (yield estimate × modal market price × category net profit ratio). It carries higher estimation variance and uncertainty than the expected yield range alone, and should be treated as an agronomic benchmark rather than a farm-specific financial audit.
5. **Known Frontend/Backend Crop Support Mismatch**: The frontend's `mockYieldData.ts` includes `wheat` and `soybean`. However, FasalSetu's backend canonical dataset intentionally supports 22 specific crops. Any request for `wheat` or `soybean` returns HTTP 422 with the list of supported crops. Either the frontend can adjust its crop selection to the supported 22 crops, or a future release may expand the canonical dataset.

### 17. Deal Transaction Status & Stage Advancement (Feature 17)

Tracks the post-acceptance lifecycle of a deal once an offer is accepted via Feature 10 (`PATCH /api/listings/{id}/offers/{offer_id}` with `action="accept"`). This transitions the listing status to `sold` and **automatically creates a `Transaction` row** in the same database transaction.

> **Status Board Scope Decision (No Money Movement)**:
> In alignment with the project's earlier Feature 7 architectural decision to explicitly exclude real payment gateways, escrow, and courier/logistics providers, this feature operates purely as an **interactive deal status board**. It manages workflow status transitions (`in_progress` -> `completed`) and display timelines, but does **not** move money or integrate with third-party logistics APIs.

> **Single-Sided Farmer Confirmation Simplification (Documented Gap)**:
> In this implementation, both manual stage advancements (`confirm_pickup` and `confirm_payment`) are performed exclusively by the listing's owning farmer. In reality, a buyer confirming payment remittance would be more trustworthy than a farmer confirming receipt; however, because no buyer-facing transaction UI exists yet in the current mobile frontend, all confirmation actions are farmer-anchored for now. When buyer transaction views are built in the future, this can be extended to two-sided handshakes without breaking the core state machine.

> **Target Dates as Display Offsets**:
> `pickup_target_date` (+2 days) and `payment_target_date` (+6 days) are set at transaction creation matching the frontend `getRelativeDate(2)` and `getRelativeDate(6)` display conventions. These represent indicative timeline targets for user interface rendering, not hard-enforced expiration deadlines.

#### Lifecycle Stages Timeline
The response contains an ordered 5-node timeline matching the mobile `TransactionStatusScreen`:
1. **`matched`**: Auto-completed at creation (the buyer's offer was accepted).
2. **`negotiated`**: Auto-completed at creation (subtext shows the agreed settlement rate, e.g. `Agreed at ₹2,520/quintal`).
3. **`logistics`**: Starts `active`. Advanced to `completed` when the farmer confirms pickup.
4. **`payment`**: Starts `pending`. Transitions to `active` once pickup completes. Advanced to `completed` when farmer confirms payment receipt.
5. **`completed`**: Starts `pending`. Transitions to `completed` simultaneously with payment confirmation, closing the transaction (`overall_status: "completed"`).

#### 2. PATCH /api/transactions/{listing_id}/advance
Advances the deal to its next operational stage.

- **Headers**: `Authorization: Bearer <farmer_jwt>`, `Content-Type: application/json`
- **Request Body**:
```json
{
  "action": "confirm_pickup"
}
```
*(Valid actions: `"confirm_pickup"`, `"confirm_payment"`)*
- **Strict Sequencing Validation (HTTP `409 Conflict`)**:
  - `confirm_payment` called while `logistics_status != "completed"` returns `409 Conflict`: `Conflict: Cannot confirm payment before pickup/logistics has been completed.`
  - `confirm_pickup` called when logistics is not `active` returns `409 Conflict`.
  - Any advancement attempted after `overall_status == "completed"` returns `409 Conflict`: `Conflict: Transaction is already completed. No further stage advancements are permitted.`
- **Role Enforcement**: Requires `farmer` role and ownership of the underlying listing (`listing.farmer_id == current_user.id`). Other roles receive `403 Forbidden`.

#### 3. GET /api/my/transactions

  - `buyer`: Returns all transactions on accepted offers submitted by the buyer (`buyer_id == current_user.id`).
  - Sorted by `created_at` descending.
- **Response (`200 OK`)**: List of `TransactionResponse` objects.

> - **Synchronous Generation vs Job Queue**: Reports are generated synchronously within the request lifecycle and transition immediately to status `"Ready"`. ## 🤖 Machine Learning & AI Details

1. **Crop Recommendation**:
   - **Algorithm**: Multi-Class `RandomForestClassifier` (`n_estimators=100`, `random_state=42`)
   - **Dataset**: Atharva Ingle Crop Recommendation Dataset (Kaggle), 2,200 rows
   - **Evaluated Test Accuracy**: **99.55%** on stratified 80/20 train/test split
   - **Input Features (7)**: `N`, `P`, `K`, `temperature`, `humidity`, `ph`, `rainfall`
   - **Output**: Top recommended crop + confidence + 2 alternative crops with confidences

2. **Farmer Q&A Assistant**:
   - **Provider**: Groq Cloud API
   - **Model**: `llama-3.1-8b-instant` (ultra-fast inference, high multilingual competency)
   - **Prompt Engineering**: Strict agricultural domain constraint, direct answer in user's query language, safety disclaimer
   - **Robustness**: 15s explicit timeout, markdown code-block stripping (` ```json `), and regex fallback to prevent 500 errors

3. **Whisper Voice Transcription (Feature 3B)**:
   - **Provider**: Groq Cloud API
   - **Model**: `whisper-large-v3-turbo` (sub-second speech-to-text inference)
   - **Timeout**: Enforced 15-second request timeout
   - **Supported Audio**: `.m4a`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.webm` (up to 25 MB)
   - **Multilingual Recognition**: Automatic language detection with optional ISO-639-1 language hint support (Hindi, English, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil, etc.)

4. **Image-based Crop Diagnosis & Vision Model Decision (Feature 5)**:
   - **Provider**: Groq Cloud API
   - **Configured Vision Model**: `qwen/qwen3.6-27b` (configured as default via `GROQ_VISION_MODEL` in `config.py` and `.env.example`)
   - **Model Tier**: **Preview-tier** (multimodal vision capabilities hosted on Groq)
   - **Contingency Note**: *If this model is deprecated, check the Groq vision documentation at [https://console.groq.com/docs/vision](https://console.groq.com/docs/vision) for its replacement before deploying.*
   - **Failure Resilience**: Groq's multimodal lineup has evolved rapidly and preview models are subject to decommissioning. The backend handles `model_decommissioned`-style errors and timeouts with a graceful HTTP 503 response (`Image diagnosis unavailable, please try again shortly`) rather than crashing or throwing an unhandled 500.
   - **Supported Image Formats & Limits**: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` up to 10 MB.

5. **AI Negotiation Advisor (Feature 11)**:
   - **Provider**: Groq Cloud API
   - **Configured Model**: `llama-3.1-8b-instant` (overridable via `GROQ_MODEL`)
   - **Strictly Advisory**: Constrained by system prompt to guidance only; zero autonomous action execution.
   - **National Mandi Benchmark Grounding**: Dynamically injects price min/max/modal aggregates across all recorded mandis as national benchmarks.
   - **Timeout & Failure Handling**: Enforced 15-second request timeout returning HTTP 503 (`Negotiation assistant unavailable, please try again shortly`) upon failure.

6. **Water Irrigation Advisory (Feature 12)**:
   - **Provider**: Open-Meteo Weather Forecast API (`https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast`)
   - **Architecture**: 100% Deterministic rule-based agronomic model (zero LLM calls or token costs).
   - **Client**: `httpx` (explicitly pinned in `requirements.txt`) with an enforced 10-second timeout.
   - **Data Grounding**: Live temperature, humidity, and rainfall combined with past 3-day and forecast 3-day precipitation, adapted to crop water consumption curves and soil drainage traits.
   - **Resilience**: Returns HTTP 503 (`{"error": "Weather data unavailable, please try again shortly"}`) if Open-Meteo encounters timeouts or errors.

7. **Yield & Harvest Estimation (Feature 13)**:
   - **Architecture**: 100% Deterministic rule-based agronomic model (zero LLM calls or token costs).
   - **Benchmark Basis**: ICAR & State Agricultural Department published yield ranges and crop-duration benchmarks across all 22 supported crops.
   - **Market Grounding**: Dynamically queries modal mandi prices from the `MarketPrice` table (filtered by state if specified).
   - **Profit Estimation Caveat**: Compounded projection (`yield × modal_price × (1 - category_cost_ratio)`) carrying higher estimation variance than yield ranges alone.
